# Spell Mechanics
---
## Spell Overuse (TN Escalation)
Whenever a caster **repeats the same spell more than once** during the same scene, encounter, or sustained situation, the **Target Number (TN) for that spell increases by +2** for each additional cast.
* 1st cast: Base TN (as listed in the spell)
* 2nd cast: Base TN +2
* 3rd cast: Base TN +4
* 4th cast: Base TN +6
* 5th cast: Base TN +8
… and so on.

> This escalation applies only to the same spell;  **Switching to a different spell does not increase TN** (unless that spell has also been used previously in the same scene).

After failing a spell, that spell cannot be cast again until a TN reset.

##### When does the TN reset? 
* The TN resets to the spell’s Base TN **when the scene ends, the encounter concludes, or the caster has had time to rest** and rebalance their magical flow.
* A **short rest (10+ minutes)** is typically enough. 


##### Why does this rule exist?
* This mechanic **prevents infinite spell-spamming**  and **maintains tension even for high-level casters** with large dice pools and HP reserves.
