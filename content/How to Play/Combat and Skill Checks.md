# Combat

## Attacking
* roll Trait + Combat Skill dice pool
* compare to enemy Defense / TN
* if success --> roll damage: 
	* Weapon Base Damage + (skill)D6
	* (6's do not explode on damage)

### Critical Hit
* Beat TN by 9+ --> double the number of (skill)D6 rolled
### Critical Fail 
* Miss TN by 5+ --> penalty or mishap
	* -2 defense, weapon jam, spell backlash, etc (DM choice)
	* free counter attack from enemy
### Armor 
* Passive Absorption 
	* Light:  1-4
	* Medium: 5-9
	* Heavy: 10-15


# Skill Checks

## How To Roll
* Check the value of your **Skill**, and its related **Trait**
* Roll a number of D6 equal to the total number of dots


### * **Keep number of dice equal to your skill value**, referring to the following..

* **0 Dot** - 
	* Keep 2 dice: Highest and Lowest result 
	+ + Trait modifier
* **1 Dot** - 
	* Keep 2 dice: Highest and Lowest result
	*  + Trait modifier
	*  + one roll of 6 will explode
* **2 Dots** - 
	* Keep 2 dice: Highest results
	*  + Trait modifier
	*  + one roll of 6 will explode
* **3 Dots** - 
	* Keep 3 dice: Highest results
	*  + Trait modifier
	* + roll two 6, they explode
* **4 Dots** - 
	* Keep 4 dice: Highest results
	* + Trait modifier
	* + roll two 6, they explode
* **5 Dots** - 
	* Keep 5 dice: Highest results
	*  + Trait modifier
	*  + roll three 6, they explode
 
---

#### • Exploding Dice •
If a kept die rolls a 6... 
* Re-roll it and add the new result to the total. • 
* Maximum explosions: Up to 3 times.

#### • Critical Success • 
* Occurs when your total exceeds the Target Number (TN) by 10 or more. 
	* Rewards: Gain a Critical Token and additional benefits (GM discretion). • 
	* Maximum Tokens: Up to 5. 

#### • Critical Failure • 
* Occurs when your total is 5 or more below the TN. • 
	* Penalties: Suffer significant setbacks or complications (GM discretion).
